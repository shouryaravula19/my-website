Welcome to My Website

I'm Shourya Ravula, and I grew up around real estate before I ever understood what an asset class was. My family invests in residential properties, and from a young age I learned firsthand how to manage properties as a landlord — collecting rent, handling repairs, and understanding what actually makes a property worth owning. That early, hands-on exposure sparked a genuine interest in real estate that has stuck with me ever since.

That interest eventually led me to my first co-op at AEW Capital, and my second at Bain Capital, where I got to see real estate investing from the other side — at the institutional level, with underwriting models, portfolio strategy, and capital markets instead of just a single rental unit. I learned a lot, and genuinely enjoyed every bit of it, which is part of why I built this page around the topic.

What stuck with me most from both experiences is how differently real estate behaves compared to a lot of other private investments. Capital isn't locked away for years with nothing to show for it in the meantime — a property can generate income almost right away, and that changes how I think about risk entirely.

On the next page, I go into more depth on why I think real estate is one of the strongest asset classes out there, along with a visualization that gets at one of the reasons why.
